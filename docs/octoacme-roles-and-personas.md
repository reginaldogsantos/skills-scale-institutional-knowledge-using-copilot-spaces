# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads oversee the quality assurance strategy, coordinate testing efforts across the team, and ensure that features meet defined acceptance criteria before release. They serve as the primary liaison between development and QA contributors, balancing thoroughness with delivery velocity.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain test strategies and plans for each project phase
- Coordinate test planning, execution, and defect management
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria in collaboration with Product and Developers
- Ensure test coverage meets project requirements and risk profiles
- Mentor and coordinate QA contributors and manual testers
- Track and report quality metrics and test results
- Identify and escalate quality risks early in the development cycle

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet user expectations
- Reduce production defects and improve customer satisfaction
- Streamline testing processes without compromising quality
- Enable fast feedback loops during development

### Typical Communication
- Test planning meetings with Product and Development
- Daily standup participation and blocker reporting
- Quality metrics dashboards and weekly QA reports
- Issue triage and defect prioritization sessions

## UX Designer / Researcher

### Role Summary
UX Designers and Researchers drive user-centered design and research efforts. They translate user needs into intuitive, usable interfaces and validate design decisions through research and testing. They collaborate closely with Product and Development to ensure the final product aligns with user expectations and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity designs
- Define information architecture and user flows
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feature prioritization and trade-offs
- Validate design solutions with users and iterate based on feedback
- Work with Developers to ensure design feasibility and implementation quality
- Maintain design systems and component libraries for consistency

### Goals
- Create intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce friction and improve user task completion rates
- Ensure alignment between user needs and business objectives
- Build a cohesive, recognizable product identity

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions with Development and Product
- User research findings and insights presentations
- Design specifications and component documentation
- Collaboration on accessibility and usability standards

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers create and maintain clear, accurate documentation that enables users, support teams, and contributors to understand and effectively use the product. They work across all project phases to ensure knowledge is captured, organized, and accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Author user guides, API documentation, and technical specifications
- Create release notes and migration guides for new versions
- Maintain and update product documentation and FAQs
- Collaborate with Developers to understand technical implementation
- Ensure documentation is accurate, accessible, and searchable
- Develop documentation standards and templates
- Support knowledge transfer between teams and project phases

### Goals
- Reduce support tickets and improve user self-service
- Accelerate onboarding for new users and team members
- Ensure consistent and clear communication across all documentation
- Enable faster troubleshooting and issue resolution

### Typical Communication
- Documentation planning and content strategy meetings
- Technical specification reviews with Developers
- Content updates in response to feature releases
- Feedback collection from support and user communities

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers plan, coordinate, and oversee the release of features and updates to production. They ensure all release criteria are met, manage deployment schedules, and facilitate communication with stakeholders. They serve as the decision point for go/no-go determinations.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain release calendars and schedules
- Coordinate release readiness across all teams (Development, QA, Ops)
- Verify all acceptance criteria, tests, and security scans are complete
- Manage release notes, documentation, and stakeholder communications
- Orchestrate deployments to staging and production environments
- Facilitate post-deployment verification and monitoring
- Support rollback procedures and incident response when needed
- Track and report release metrics and deployment success

### Goals
- Deliver releases on schedule with minimal risk
- Ensure smooth deployments with zero or minimal downtime
- Maintain clear communication with all stakeholders throughout release cycle
- Enable rapid response to deployment issues

### Typical Communication
- Release kickoff and readiness review meetings
- Daily release coordination standups (if in active release window)
- Pre-deployment checklists and go/no-go decisions
- Post-deployment verification reports and stakeholder announcements
